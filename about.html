About Connect Tutors SA

Organisation's history

Upon research we have noticed that a lot of students who struggle with their academics often don't have access to tutors as they are expensive and it is hard for some of the students to access tutors.

Vision

ConnectTutors SA's vision is to provide a platform for all students to access free educational support, it also aims to empower them to achieve academic success and for them to reach their full potential.

Mission

Connect tutors SA's mission is to connect students with suitable tutors based on their learning needs and preffered learning techniques. This website provides free tutoring opportunities and access to educationalresources and encouraging students to improve their academic perfomance, develop confidence and take pride in their studies. It also promotes an inclusive environment where students feel comfortable asking for help.
